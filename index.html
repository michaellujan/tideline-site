How it plays

Beams of colored light cross a small board. Tap mirrors, splitters, prisms, and combiners to turn them, and drag pieces from the tray onto the board, until every receiver glows in exactly its color. There are no timers and no way to fail. The campaign has 120 levels across eight chapters, and Daily Light gives everyone the same new puzzle every day.
