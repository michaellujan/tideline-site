Privacy policy

Tideline · last updated September 26, 2026

Tideline does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own.

  • The app never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location, and has no sign-in.
  • Your progress (stars, Daily Light results and streaks, hints), settings, and a record of what you have bought are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions. The app asks Apple which items you own so it can unlock them; we never see your payment details.
  • The app contains no third-party code that collects data: no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • When you tap Share after a Daily Light puzzle, the text you share (your stars, moves, streak, and the puzzle's colors) goes wherever you send it. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.

Questions: see the support page.
